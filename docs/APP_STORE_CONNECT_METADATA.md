## App Store Description

### Promotional Text (170 characters)
Connect with your neighborhood. Discover local plans, join community discussions, and build meaningful connections with people nearby.

### Description (4000 characters max)

Commons is a neighborhood-focused social platform that helps you connect with your local community through shared plans, events, and conversations.

**Discover Your Neighborhood**
Find plans and events happening near you, from coffee meetups to community gatherings. Browse by neighborhood or explore new areas to discover what's happening locally.

**Create & Join Plans**
Start your own plans or join existing ones. Whether it's a weekend brunch, a neighborhood cleanup, or a casual hangout, Commons makes it easy to organize and participate in local activities.

**Community Discussions**
Engage in neighborhood forums, share local recommendations, and stay informed about what matters in your community. Connect with neighbors who share your interests.

**Safe & Welcoming**
Built with safety in mind, Commons is an invite-only community focused on creating authentic, meaningful connections. Robust moderation tools and user controls ensure a respectful environment.

**Key Features:**
• Discover nearby plans and events in real-time
• Create and manage your own community plans
• Join neighborhood-specific forums and discussions
• Share photos and updates with your local community
• Real-time chat with plan participants
• Search for local venues and places
• Personalized notifications for plans you care about
• Full account privacy controls and data deletion

**Your Privacy Matters**
We're committed to protecting your privacy. Your data is yours, and you can delete your account at any time. We don't sell your information or show third-party ads.

Commons is perfect for anyone looking to:
• Meet neighbors and make local friends
• Discover events and activities nearby
• Build a stronger sense of community
• Stay connected with neighborhood happenings
• Organize group activities and gatherings

Join Commons and rediscover the joy of local connection.
